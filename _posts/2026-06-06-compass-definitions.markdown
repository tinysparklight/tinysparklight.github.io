Today I am going to share definitions of some *big important* things that I have come to accept as true. I do not want to pretend that these are of my own creation, they are in fact ideas I read somewhere (sorry I don't remember the sources) and that have stuck with me since. These have been loitering in my head again since a few weeks now, spurred by some recent reading and introspection. They are by no means prescriptive, but feel free to experiment with these ideas and see if there is any truth in them for you. 

I might feel the need to evolve these over time, when I find that they are no longer able to do justice to my observations or experiences. I will also add to this list when I am compelled to define another *big important* life thing.

**Success**

Success is having the freedom to do what you want with your time, energy and attention.

**Love**

Love is a verb and it involves giving. It's not an abstract concept. Being willing to give away your time, attention, energy, material things to someone are not just acts of love, they are love itself. This means that love in every relationship needs work, just saying the words is not enough.
