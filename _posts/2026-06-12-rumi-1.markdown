I have always loved Rumi, not in a "oh-I-have-read-everything-by-him" way, but more like a "I-am-familiar-with-his-work-and-have-loved-the-stuff-I-have-come-across" way. Even my WhatsApp status, when status used to be just a line of text, for the last several years, has been a quote attributed to him, "Love is the bridge between you and everything". 

Rumi was a 13th-century Persian poet, and Sufi mystic who wrote on spirituality and the oneness of humanity.

A few days back I came to know about a book of his - *'Gold'*, a translated work by Haleh Liza Gafori, and it was recommended as an excellent and deeply enjoyable translation. I immediately ordered it and it arrived in my mail yesterday (yay technology!). It's a very thin volume, but I am taking my time to savor it slowly and deliberately. I have also decided to share the ones I love in this space as a series, this being part 1. Here's a really short one to get us going.

> Ferment like wine

> in the barrel of your body.

What a whimsical yet profound sentence...
