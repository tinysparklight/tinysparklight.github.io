Starting to compile what I hope becomes a compendium of things I consider 'truisms' when it comes to parenting.

> I want to be more present when with my toddler and try to keep in mind that she is never trying to give me a hard time, she is behaving the way she is because she is having a hard time. Let me be curious and find out why, and help her.

---

> One of the best things parents can do for their children is look after their own health.

---

> Let your children overhear you saying beautiful things about them. It becomes the voice they carry in their hearts for when they need it most.

---

> "Every child needs at least one adult who is irrationally crazy about him or her." - Dr. Urie Bronfenbrenner

---

> You either face your demons or they raise your children.

---

> In the end I am the only one who can give my children a happy mother who loves life.
