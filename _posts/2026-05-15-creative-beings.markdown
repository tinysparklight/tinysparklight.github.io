I recently heard Mom say, "...that's fine, but that's because I am not creative." I passionately disagree. I think most of us, and this includes yours truly, have been fed a wrong notion of 'creativity' by family, society and culture.

I believe that we are all beings with three faculties: mind, intellect and tendencies accumulated over time, also known as sanskaras. Mind is the one that creates thoughts and feels emotions, intellect is the one that discerns and our tendencies 'tend' to rule us, unless we use our conscious mind and intellect to override them.

I am slowly coming to realize that we are all creative beings, and not beings who are selectively creative. Notice how I said that the mind *creates* thoughts - we literally have a factory of thoughts and emotions in our head. We create thought, thus we are creators by nature. We all channel our attention and energy in different directions, thus employing our creativity in various avenues which determines which parts of our creative muscle we grow. This does not look the same for any two persons and so comparing people on the basis of creativity seems like a moot point.

These are obviously just my opinions, realized recently, and I reserve the right to change them, haha.

While I was reading the fourth volume of *The Unselected Journals of Emma M. Lion*, I was caught by surprise when I read the author describing a painting by an artist as *a study of a landscape*. This idea of calling a painting *a study* really stuck with me. I think it's supportive of the above ideas - a painting is the artist's study of the subject, a very personal perspective of the artist who is exercising their creative muscle, the one that each one of us has, to project what they have gleaned from the subject onto a canvas.
