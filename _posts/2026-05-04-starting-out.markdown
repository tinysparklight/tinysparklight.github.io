Hello! This is my first post. Rubber has finally hit the road, pen has been finally been put to paper, and so on... I have been meaning to start sharing my (random, due to my varied interests,) thoughts online since many years but never had the courage to do so. Recently I started listening to The Gifts of Imperfection by Brené Brown audiobook (a book I had purchased years ago, but never got around to listening to... you see, there is a theme here, this deservers a post of its own - taking note of it). So far I have only reached the second guidepost in the list of ten, `Letting Go of Perfection`. The main theme of the book seems to be Authenticity and owning your story. It's about courage, compassion and connection... so I have mustered the courage to take this leap. I am going to be writing for myself, this blog is not meant for anyone else really (though I hope my husband and sister get around to reading some of my posts, would love that :). It is probably going to be a microblogging thing starting out, I feel. And I say that probably to bring down the stakes, so that this doesn't feel too scary. It might turn into something else over time, who knows!

I have spent the entire day today trying to understand how to set this whole thing up - I wanted the blog to have a really cool theme (lol), but I had no inkling of what Github Pages or Jekyll is. I asked Gemini, "help me setup a personal blog using Github Pages and Jekyll with 'so and so' theme" and I would end up hitting roadblock after roadblock. And then, I stumbled upon this theme that someone else created for their blog and very kindly, open-sourced. So, here we are.

Will leave it at that for today, it's pretty late, I am tired, need to wake up early tomorrow for daycare drop-off... À bientôt :)
