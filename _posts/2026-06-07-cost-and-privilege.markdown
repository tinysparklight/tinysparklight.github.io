I used to think that people who get to work on things that they are passionate about are very lucky. Slowly as I mature, I am realizing that luck is rarely the case.

I strongly feel that being able to work on something that you feel deeply about or connected to is a privilege, and I mean the fortunate kind of privilege, not the entitled kind. This privilege is usually well-earned, otherwise it's not long-lasting. If you are not prepared to pay the cost, don't be surprised or upset if you don't get the privilege.

By cost I mean doing the work to invest in yourself. Work on those skills. Then do the work that generates proof of your interest and skills, create a portfolio. Don't wait for permission, or hang around in self-doubt. Have the agency to just go and do it, even if no one is looking or asking you. The best part? It's actually much more doable in the field of software, which I am familiar with.

So go, do the things that move you closer to the kind of professional you need to be so that you can actually be impactful towards that mission that is stirring all this excitement in you. It's okay if you are not there yet today. Remember life is long and you know you have the capacity to work hard and improve, so work hard and good luck!
